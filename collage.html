My Collage Board

Tap Search Pinterest or Search Google to go find pictures, save the ones you love to your phone, then come back here and upload them — your board is saved automatically while you're away, so nothing is lost. Tap a piece, then Cut out to remove the background, and arrange everything on a board that's nearly as tall as your phone screen. It doubles as a moodboard. Pinch a piece with two fingers to make it bigger or smaller. A piece only starts moving once your finger really moves, so just tapping it won't nudge it. No border, no limits — pieces can hang right off the edges, and there's no limit on how many fit. If one wanders off where you can't grab it, Gather pieces brings them all back. Your saved boards live below in My collages and save in this browser only, like your blog and calendar — for keepers, tap Export picture and save it to your phone. Note: the export still crops to the board rectangle, so hanging-off bits won't show in it.
