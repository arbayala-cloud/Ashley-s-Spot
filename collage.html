My Collage Board

Tap Search Pinterest or Search Google to go find pictures, save the ones you love to your phone, then come back here and upload them — your board is saved automatically while you're away, so nothing is lost. Tap a piece, then Cut out to remove the background, and arrange everything on a board that's nearly as tall as your phone screen. It doubles as a moodboard. Pinch a piece with two fingers to make it bigger or smaller. A piece only starts moving once your finger really moves, so just tapping it won't nudge it. The board has a clean black border, and pieces stay inside it — they can snuggle right up to the edge but never cross it. For clothes on a plain background, Magic wand cuts it instantly with no download at all. Want it cut by hand instead? Tap a piece, then Trace cut by hand, and draw a loop around just what you want to keep — or around what you want gone. If an older board has a piece that wandered off, Gather pieces brings them all back. Your saved boards live below in My collages and save in this browser only, like your blog and calendar — for keepers, tap Export picture and save it to your phone. Tap a cutout piece, then Save to closet, and it waits in My closet for any board — your own little Polyvore stash. Tap Add words to put words right on your set, Polyvore-style. The Browse taps open Pinterest searches for dresses, tops, shoes, bags, jewelry, beauty, and accessories — save what you love, then upload it here. Export picture saves exactly what you see on the board.
